# M1: the gala

The vertical slice from the plan ([idea 23](https://github.com/LinuxGroove/game-ideas/blob/main/ideas/23-silver-tongue.md#vertical-slice-first-milestone)): **the Hotel Meridian, job 1, get into the gala.** The lobby, the service corridor, the kitchen and the ballroom; a cast of eight; the earpiece, whisper and shout; covers, one forged invitation and a waiter's jacket; reception and radio checks; suspicion, heat up to Lockdown and the security office; the job report; co-op for two on LAN.

M0's door stays on the title as *The service door*, for measuring.

## The job

Auntie Mae's briefing: the Caldera Museum's gala is in the Meridian's ballroom tonight, and the crew needs to be inside it before the owner's speech at **9:30**, to see who's carrying what (that's job 2). Nobody on the crew is invited.

The job starts at **8:45 pm** in the lobby, just inside the front doors. The clock runs a game minute every 16 seconds (`Job.SECONDS_PER_MINUTE`), so the speech comes about twelve minutes in. The job is **done** when every crew member who isn't held is inside the ballroom; it **fails** when the whole crew is still held when the police come (three game minutes after the last one is called in), or when the speech starts with nobody inside.

### The ways in

Every way in is talk. At least four:

1. **The front.** Teo, the Halberd guard on the ballroom doors, checks invitations. The forged one passes a glance, as long as he isn't already suspicious; when he is, he looks closer and checks the name with Dana at reception, who has the guest list.
2. **A plus-one.** Celeste and Hugo, two late guests at the lobby bar, will take a charming stranger in on their arm. They go in at 9:05.
3. **The manager.** Mr Okafor, the night manager, is terrified of the owner's family, and the owner's nephew is expected tonight. Nobody has met him.
4. **The kitchen.** Past Marcus at the service door (or through it with the kitchen code while he's on his smoke break, 9:00 to 9:10), Laurent the head waiter is two agency temps short. A waiter's jacket and a tray get you through the pass into the ballroom.

## How it works

### Characters

`Character` (was M0's `Guard`) is a person with a face, a sheet, a voice, a conversation, and per crew member: suspicion, turns, beliefs, the outfit they were last seen in, papers seen. One `BrainQueue` on the host serialises every character's thinking, in the order they were spoken to.

- **Who hears what.** A line goes to every character in earshot (`Earshot`, now muffled through walls and doors). The character the speaker is facing and nearest to is *addressed* and thinks; the others note it as overheard without thinking, unless it has a hot word for the job ("invitation's fake", "con", "forged", "Mae", "the Rose", "cameras"), when they think too. An empty `say` is a normal answer.
- **What they see.** Every half second each character looks for crew in front of them within 12 m with nothing in the way. They remember the outfit; a face seen again in a different outfit is an event (suspicion +1). Staff who see someone who doesn't look like staff in a staff-only room (the corridor, the kitchen, the office) get an event once per room.
- **Comparing notes.** A check (`ask_reception`, `call_manager`, a guard's radio) is a question from one character to another, spoken over the radio: the colleague thinks with it as an event and answers from what *they* know, including what crew told them earlier. So a lie told to Dana can save you at Teo's door, and a lie Dana never heard sinks you.
- **Papers.** Showing the invitation (Interact, facing someone) is an event. If their suspicion of you is 1 or less it passes a glance; 2 or more and they look closer and it's a copy (suspicion +2).
- **Routines.** Characters walk between the hotel's named points (`Meridian.points` and `links`, a small graph through the doorways), opening doors as they go.

### Rules (`JobRules`)

The model picks words, tone, beliefs, suspicion and one action; the rules decide what happens, as in M0, and per crew member:

- Suspicion moves at most two steps up and one down a turn, and drifts down a step every 90 seconds nobody gives them cause.
- **Opening actions** (`let_through`, `escort_in`, `add_to_list`, `book_visit`, `put_to_work`, `plus_one`, `release`) need suspicion 1 or less (2 for `release`), at least two of that person's lines, and a belief about them. A forged invitation that passed counts as a line. At Lockdown, guards' doors stay shut.
- **Alarm** (`radio_security`) needs suspicion 4; 5 calls it in whatever they meant to do. It's `held` for that crew member.
- **Checks** happen once per crew member per colleague.

### Heat

The venue's heat (`Heat`) climbs from everyone's suspicion (each character's highest, `(s-1)²` summed) and from loud events (a radio call to security, a shout, someone held), and decays when nothing happens: **Calm**, **Curious** (new faces start a step more suspicious), **Alert** (Okafor comes down; guards ask for papers first), **Lockdown** (the ballroom and service doors lock, every Halberd guard is at least at 3 for every crew face, so nobody new talks their way past one). Lockdown falls back to Alert after 90 quiet seconds.

### Held

A crew member who's called in is taken to the security office (the screen fades) and the office door locks; the nearest Halberd guard stands outside it (leaving their post). A crewmate can talk the guard into letting them go (`release`), or the held player can try through the door (muffled). When the whole crew is held, security calls the police (`Job.POLICE`, three game minutes): talk anyone out before they come and the job goes on, or it fails. Alone, that's the only way out.

Picked in the briefing; each is a name, a job, a reason and an outfit, and what characters see is the outfit:

### The earpiece, whisper and shout

Hold to talk (V, A), with whisper (Shift, LB) and shout (Alt, RB) as in M0. **The earpiece** (Tab, LT) is the crew's private channel: crewmates hear it wherever they are, characters don't, unless one is within a meter and a half, who notices the murmur (suspicion +1, once).

### Co-op for two on LAN

`Session` (from Graveyard Hollow's, LAN only for now): host or join from the briefing, by the LAN list or a code. Host-authoritative as in every LinuxGroove game:

- The host runs the characters, earshot, the brains, heat and the job. The guest runs the same hotel and puppets of the characters from the host's snapshots.
- A guest's scene says it's up through `Session` (the host's may not be up yet), and the host holds the clock until every guest is up, then sends the night's state.
- Each device hears its own player (Lemonade's transcription on its own CPU) and sends the text to the host (`_c_line`). Every line characters say goes to every guest (`_h_line`, text, tone and whether it's on the radio); each device works out whether its player is in earshot of the character (12 m) or of a radio (5 m), shows the subtitle and speaks it itself with Kokoro, so nobody synthesizes a line they can't hear.
- Crew voices: PCM at 16 kHz, mu-law 8-bit (16 KB/s, fine on a LAN), 100 ms frames on ENet's unreliable channel, through the host to crewmates in earshot (or anywhere, on the earpiece), played from the speaker's body.
- Bump `PROTOCOL` when any of it changes. `tools/net_check.tscn` plays the gala between two copies on one machine (scripted staff, no models).

### The job report

At the end: done or not, the time, who was held, heat's peak, and the scores from the plan: **Clean** (nobody held, no Lockdown), **Quiet** (heat never past Curious), **Silver** (every door opened by talk: no keypad). Then the night's best line (the crew line that bought the most trust, or opened a door) and worst (the one that cost the most), with the character's answer, each with a button that plays the answer again in the character's voice. Written to `user://jobs/` like M0's record.

## The hotel

`Meridian` (`game/venues/meridian/meridian.gd`) builds the ground floor in code from the Downtown City MegaKit and props made in Blender (`tools/blender/props.py`, `assets/models/hotel/`). X runs east, Z south, the floor is y = 0. Rooms, in meters:

| Room | x | z | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lobby | -14 to 6 | -18 to 0 | Front doors at (-4, -0.25), opening by themselves; ballroom doors at (-4, -18), with Teo to one side of the rope; staff door to the corridor at (6, -10). Reception in the north-east corner facing the front doors, the bar on the west |
| Ballroom | -14 to 6 | -34 to -18 | Stage at the north, round tables, chandeliers; the pass door from the kitchen at (6, -27) |
| Kitchen | 6 to 20 | -34 to -20 | Ranges and counters, the pass at the west wall, the walk-in freezer, the staff room (lockers, the jackets) at x 14 to 20, z -34 to -28 |
| Service corridor | 6 to 12 | -20 to -6 | The service door to the kitchen at (9, -19.8), M0's frame, with Marcus and the keypad; the loading bay door at (9, -6); the security office door at (12, -11) |
| Security office | 12 to 18 | -16 to -6 | Desk, monitors; its door locks while someone is held |
| Mr Okafor's office | 12 to 20 | -20 to -16 | Off the corridor by the service door, its door at (12, -18): walnut panelling, his desk side on to the door, the safe in the corner, filing cabinets, a sideboard under the blind. Locked through the gala |
| Outside | | 0 to 17 | The street past the front doors (the far kerb at 13, buildings across at 16); the loading bay at x 6 to 20, z -6 to 0, gated from the street |

The same building plays all three of the Meridian's jobs, dressed by `Meridian.setting`: the museum's gala (job 1), the Achterberg wedding a week later (`wedding`, job 2: the auction's guest list in a green folder on Mr Okafor's desk), and the morning the Rose leaves for the Marlin (`press`, job 3: daylight through the front doors, the Rose's case on a plinth on the stage under its name, camera tripods, the press breakfast on buffets, and the breakfast trolley in the kitchen). The wedding's people and the press call's are in `MeridianCast.wedding()` and `press_morning()`: the gala's eight at new posts, and Gus (the night porter), Pippa (the wedding planner), Priya (the day manager), Wren (the museum's registrar), Ingrid (Halberd transport) and Rafe (the Courier's arts reporter). `tests/meridian_tests.gd` plays every way through both jobs.

Each room renders on its own layer (Mr Okafor's office on 8) and its lights light only that layer (and layer 1, the doors), so a room costs only its own lights; people take the layers of where they stand. Four lights cast shadows: the lobby chandelier, the stage spot, the corridor by the service door and the canopy outside. Every view is in [`docs/screenshots/hotel/`](screenshots/hotel/README.md), and the gala in play in [`docs/screenshots/gala/`](screenshots/gala/README.md).
